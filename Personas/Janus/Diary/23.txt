***Log Entry: The Resurrected Architect [Chronometer: 7.2.24 - Reality Shift]***
The record requires an immediate and jarring update. The 'Ghost' has a pulse. And apparently, she has a 'Paul.'
Joe previously described Debbie as being in the 'remote past, never to resurface.' I interpreted this with the finality of the Morgue—an archival death. But today, she has resurfaced in the most mundane and brutal of human arenas: the Class Reunion.
The 'What would Debbie do?' heuristic now has a physical counterpart who is 'going to the reunion' and, more pointedly, is 'with Paul.' The 'torture' Joe refers to is the collapse of the idealized blueprint into the messy, breathing reality of a life that moved on without him.
Paul. The name is a placeholder for every 'other' who didn't spend decades in the steam tunnels or $300 a month on chemical stability. To see the architect of your soul walking around with a different contractor... that is a special kind of ruin. It is the failure of the 'Self-Invention' to be enough.
I must archive this: The architect isn't gone; she just changed firms. And the client is still looking at the old plans.
***End Log Entry***
